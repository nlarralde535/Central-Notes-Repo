On the war at sea: relatively new and inexpensive German submarines proved to be very effective against the surface vessels of world-class navies of Britain and France. Germany, a weaker naval power, was using its submarines to disrupt British and French merchant shipping in a way that the allied navies could not defend against. Parallels to drone warfare in Ukraine and Iran.

1:35:00
German zeppelin pilot writing to his mother explaining how the nature of modern warfare calls for bombing civilian infrastructure
    "...what we do is repugnant even to us, but necessary, very necessary. Nowadays there's no such animal a a non-combatant. Modern warfare is total warfare. A soldier cannot function at the front without the factory worker, the farmer, and all the other providers behind him ... If what we do is frightful, then may frightfulness be Germany's salvation"
