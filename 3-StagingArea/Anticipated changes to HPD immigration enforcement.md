# Description 
### Point 0 
For context: the ICE 287g program establishes partnerships with local law enforcement agencies for the purpose of extending immigration enforcement authorities to officers/personnel within those agencies according to one of 3 models: 
 - the Jail Enforcement model
 - the Task Force model
 - the Warrant Service Officer model
### Point 1
Texas Senate Bill 8 passed on June 20 2025 and effective January 1, 2026  mandates that all Texas sheriffs in counties with >100,000 residents enter into  287g agreements with ICE by December 1, 2026.
Harris County currently has no 287g agreement in place. 
However, in 2008 Sheriff Tommy Thomas previously entered Harris County into a 287g agreement under the Jail Enforcement Model, and Sheriff Ed Gonzalez ended the agreement in 2017.
 - [[2026-05-18-075217]]
### Point 2
In November 2025 Two DPS divisions entered into a 287g agreement with ICE under the Task Force Mode : the highway patrol and criminal investigations divisions
The Task Force Model allows officers to enforce limited immigration authority while performing routine police duties.
This mean any State Trooper you see might effectively be a deputized ICE agent.

 - https://www.texasobserver.org/texas-dps-287g-ice-trump-abbott/
 - https://www.dallasnews.com/news/immigration/2025/11/04/texas-dps-troopers-will-have-immigration-enforcement-powers-under-new-agreements-with-ice/
### Point 3
Earlier this month a lawsuit was filed against the City of Houston by a Honduran man who was allegedly detained by HPD for 2 hours while waiting for ICE to arrive and detain him.

What plans is the city council considering when it comes to increased immigration enforcement on the part of HPD?
