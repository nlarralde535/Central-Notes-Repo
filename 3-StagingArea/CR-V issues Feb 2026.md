# Customer States:

- diagnostic code P0505
- while in Park, the idle RPMs will either bounce between 1000 - 1200, or hold steady around 1250
- Also while in park, the RPMs bounce as the accelerator is pressed. 
- While in Drive the RPMs intermittently surge while coasting in 1st gear (maybe also in 2nd, hard to tell.)
