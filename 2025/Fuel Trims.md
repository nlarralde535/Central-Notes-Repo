# Note on understanding fuel trims

### [Schrodinger's Box Fuel Trims](https://youtu.be/5WnM_NsOtd8?si=IR8qPCqzpjj_38xw)

a:bc - O2 sensor is like a battery that want to output about 1V, but the presence of Oxygen molecules inhibits the voltage output 

13:50 - STFT or LTFT going positive (+%) is a response by the ECM to a lean condition, (i.e. maybe unmetered air entering the engine), and going negative (-%) is a response by the ECM to a rich condition (I.e. too much fuel in the engine or not enough air)
