Intermittent no start no crank
This article [here](https://www.2carpros.com/questions/honda-crv-2003-honda-crv-2003-honda-crv-sometimes-wont-start-no-cran) suggests the following 
```
From your descrpition of the symptoms, it seems the starter solenoid is not getting any power interminttently. The most likely cause would be the igntion switch or the starter relay. Try swapping the starter relay to see if the problem continues.
```

This [video](https://youtu.be/wqz6fJqicCc?si=rnNLNYHJNMDXdUVs) suggests ensuring the screws on the under-hood ignition fuse and the fuse box itself are tightened down well.
