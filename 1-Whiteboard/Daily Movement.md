# What to do when we're away from our home gym
## Library
Squats 
 - Assisted Pistol Squats 
 - Cossack Squats (arms out)
Isometrics
 - Horse Stance
 - Archer Pushups
 - Cheerleader Holds
 - Pancakes 
 Sixth Movement 
 - Back Bridge Rotations
 - Bear Crawls
 - Leg swings + Arm shakes
 - kicks
 - Hinge Jumps

 - 1-Arm plank belly openers

3. Leg Swings + Cheerleader Holds

5. Reverse Nordic Curls + Pancakes
