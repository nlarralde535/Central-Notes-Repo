# 2-16-25
Notes pertaining to the objectives/purpose of the Growth Chapter QA initiative
 - We should NOT attempt to define a top-down QA process for the entire organization. Rather we should consider the ideal QA process that we would like to see our individual teams implement, and use that as a basis for defining a common process: bottom-up vs. top-down
 - COVER: introduce the ISTQB as the source of the vocabulary I use when talking about software testing, and test process improvement 
 - COVER: ISTQB vocab (as done at the start of the CTM: Test Process Improvement in an Agile Environment video)
 - COVER: the IDEAL model and where Growth Chapter QA fits in it
 - Given that the Growth Chapter QA is meant to produce a practical mechanism for implementing the stated goals of 100% automation test coverage of all Happy Paths, we have an opportunity to make test automation part of the Definition Of Done for every user story, which necessarily will increase the scope of every story, and thus reduce the number of stories that can be completed in a given sprint. 
 - QUESTION: What % of stories have test coverage? What % of that coverage is automated? 
 - QUESTION: How do you recall work that was done in previous sprints? How do you recall how to test a particular feature/module that you have not worked with recently? 


# 2-15-25
Notes from reading the Advanced Level Syllabus - Test Manager (2012)
 - The scope of the Growth Chapter QA is the entire WebPros organization.
 - The goals and objectives of the Growth Chapter QA are establishing a unified QA process across all WebPros companies, which include a standard for full automated test coverage of all Happy Paths
 - In terms of the IDEAL model (Initiate, Diagnose, Establish, Act, Learn), the Growth Chapter QA initiative is in the Diagnose and Establish stages. 
 - In general, on DUCK we use a requirements-based testing strategy
