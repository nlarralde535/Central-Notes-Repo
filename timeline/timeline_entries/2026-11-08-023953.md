# Arab News
"At 11:00 pm (2000 GMT), the Khor Mor gas field came under a terrorist attack," the regional natural resources and electricity authorities said in a statement, without elaborating.

A Kurdish security source told AFP that an "explosive-laden drone" hit the field.

The Khor Mor complex, which supplies most of Kurdistan's power stations and is owned by the United Arab Emirates firm Dana Gas, has been hit several times in recent years.

Omed Ahmed, the regional electricity authority spokesperson, said the attack against Khor Mor halted the gas supply.

Authorities said they were taking all necessary measures to reduce the impact of the attack on the region's power grid.

There was no immediate claim of responsibility.
