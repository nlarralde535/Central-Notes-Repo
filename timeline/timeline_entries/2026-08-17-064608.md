# Raleigh News & Observer
A man’s death in downtown Raleigh on Saturday is “being investigated as a suicide,” according to the Raleigh Police Department. Medical staff and law enforcement responded to 318 S. McDowell St. on Saturday, where a man was found hanging from a tree in a parking lot about a half-block from Nash Square, according to a Raleigh Police Department news release.

In the news release, the Raleigh Police Department said it wanted to “take a moment to acknowledge a disturbing post that is currently being shared regarding this investigation.” “We understand that this loss may be deeply painful for those who knew and cared about the individual,” according to a statement from the Raleigh Police Department. “We ask everyone to please be mindful of the family and friends who are grieving and to avoid sharing unverified information or speculation while investigators continue their work.” The Raleigh Police Department has not responded, as of 4 p.m. Sunday, to a request for additional details about what indicates that this death is a suicide and why foul play is not suspected.
