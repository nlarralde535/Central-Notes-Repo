# WGNO New Orleans 
The St. Tammany Parish Sheriff’s Office announced Friday, Aug. 21, the arrest of an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agent.

According to deputies, 58-year-old Thomas Bailey was taken into custody after allegedly entering his ex-girlfriend’s apartment without permission. 

She was not home at the time, according to deputies.

Bailey was booked into the St. Tammany Parish Correctional Center on a charge of unauthorized entry of an inhabited dwelling.

“Regardless of where you work or what position you hold, the law applies equally to everyone,” Sheriff Bret Ibert said. “When someone enters another person’s residence without permission, we are going to investigate the circumstances and take the appropriate action based on the facts of the case.”
