## CSIS article

At approximately 7:00 AM local time, the United States and Israel began conducting extensive strikes against a wide range of targets in Iran. The strikes have been dubbed Operation Epic Fury by the United States and Operation Roaring Lion by Israel.

President Trump’s TruthSocial address outlined four military objectives in Operation Epic Fury—(1) preventing Iran from acquiring a nuclear weapon, (2) destroying its missile arsenal and production sites, (3) degrading its proxy networks, and (4) annihilating its navy—alongside a desired political outcome of regime change from within. Trump framed the strikes as a counterproliferation operation and direct consequence of Iran’s refusal to renounce nuclear ambitions despite three rounds of negotiations.

Operations Epic Fury and Roaring Lion are likely the start of a prolonged conflict with Iran. The U.S.-Israel coalition has moved beyond proliferation targets and is seeking to destroy the Iranian government entirely. This conflict has already spread across the Middle East, involving strikes in more than half a dozen countries within the first few hours.

U.S. and Israeli strikes may have significantly reduced the risk of short-term Iranian proliferation, but kinetic strikes may introduce new types of proliferation risks. Iran still possesses 400 kilograms of 60 percent enriched uranium, and the exact location of that nuclear material remains unknown.
