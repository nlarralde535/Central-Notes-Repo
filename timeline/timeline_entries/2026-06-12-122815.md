# News West 9 Midland

An hours-long standoff took place after a deadly mass shooting in the City of Midland on Friday morning.

A few hours after the standoff began, Mayor Lori Blong announced that the suspect was dead, a victim was killed, and 10 others were injured.

"We can confirm that the active shooter situation is resolved and the shooter is now deceased," Blong said during a briefing.

Around 2 p.m., Texas Department of Public Safety officials identified the suspect as 45-year-old Victor Mata Villarreal, of Odessa. They said he had been wanted for attempted capital murder after he fired shots at a Midland police officer during a chase on Wednesday"

According to a statement released by the DPS, the cause of death of the shooter, identified as Victor Villreal, 45, of Odessa was reportedly found to be a "self-inflicted gunshot wound" according to preliminary findings.
