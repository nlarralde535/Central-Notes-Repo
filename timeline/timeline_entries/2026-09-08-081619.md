# KPLC 7 Louisiana
A man currently running for police chief in the Village of Elizabeth has been arrested after being accused of raping two 13-year-olds.

Sheriff Doug Hebert says Allen Parish detectives recently received a complaint in early August 2026 that Christopher Paul, 48, had raped two girls in 2005 to 2006.

After conducting interviews with victims and witnesses, an arrest warrant was issued for Paul. He later turned himself in on Tuesday, Sept. 8, and was booked on two counts of second-degree rape.
