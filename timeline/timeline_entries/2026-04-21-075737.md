++TITLE++
Virginia 2026 Congressional Redistricting vote

++DESCRIPTION++
 Virginians have passed the constitutional amendment that would allow a mid-decade redrawing of congressional districts by the Democratic majority in the Virginia General Assembly. See election results for the April 21 referendum. Several outstanding court cases have challenged the legality of this amendment. The Supreme Court of Virginia heard arguments on the first of these cases on April 27.
 
The amendment was proposed by Democratic state legislators, and would allow the Virginia General Assembly to redraw congressional districts outside of the normal 10-year redistricting process. This was part of a national Democratic response to Republican-led redistricting efforts in other states, kicked off by President Trump's encouragement to draw maps that are more favorable for the GOP before midterm elections this November. With majorities in the House of Delegates and State Senate, Virginia Democrats would be able to redraw the Commonwealth's U.S. House of Representatives districts to give their party an advantage.
