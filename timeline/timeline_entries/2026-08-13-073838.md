# Stars and Stripes 
The aircraft carrier USS George Washington will be sent to the Arabian Sea to replace USS Abraham Lincoln, which has been deployed for more than 250 days.

A Navy official confirmed the move to Stars and Stripes. It was first reported Thursday by The Wall Street Journal.

George Washington, the Navy’s only forward-deployed aircraft carrier, is based in Japan.

The decision to send Washington to the region was part of a regularly scheduled deployment. The change comes amid reports of sailors aboard Lincoln struggling with the strain of the lengthy deployment, including reports of attempts by some crew members to jump overboard.
