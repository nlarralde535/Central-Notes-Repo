# Albawaba
At least seven people were killed, and 40 others were injured in strikes that targeted a market in Taiz Province, Yemen’s Ministry of Health and Environment said Sunday.

Local Yemeni news agency, Saba, announced Sunday that five children were among those injured in the attack on Taiz's market.

The ministry, which belongs to the Houthi group, accused Saudi Arabia of carrying out the deadly attack on the market in the Al-Taiziyah district, describing the attack as a "violation of international and humanitarian law and part of a broader pattern of attacks against civilians and infrastructure".

However, despite the Houthis blaming the Saudi Air Forces for carrying out the strikes, the Yemeni Air Force announced targeting Houthi logistical and military reinforcements across several axes, including the Al-Sitteen area north of Taiz city.

Government forces and warplanes also targeted Houthi reinforcements and equipment at the Al-Dhakra and Mawiyah junctions east of the city, alongside strikes on the Al-Ahkum and Hayfan fronts, where military sources reported the neutralization of field commanders following aerial attacks.

Yemen is witnessing fierce clashes between the Houthi group and the internationally recognized Yemeni government forces. Houthis are backed by Iran, while the Yemeni government is backed by Saudi Arabia.

The latest wave of fighting started after a Houthi ground offensive in coastal areas, using missiles and drones and taking control of key cities overlooking the Bab al-Mandab Strait. 

The Yemeni government believes that the Houthis' pursuit of the strait is linked to a regional agenda aimed at influencing shipping traffic and controlling one of the most important maritime routes
