# KHOU 11
Baytown police are investigating allegations that one of their officers misused a license plate reader system, according to Chief John Stringer.

Stringer said the department received complaints involving the Flock License Plate Reader system and has already reviewed the concerns.

He confirmed an internal affairs investigation is underway to look into possible policy violations.

Stringer also authorized a separate criminal investigation into “potential criminal misconduct.”
