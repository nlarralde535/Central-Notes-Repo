++TITLE++
US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee comments that Israel can "have it all" in reference to land in the Middle East 

 ++DESCRIPTION++
Politico: Huckabee made the comments in an interview with conservative commentator Tucker Carlson that aired Friday. Carlson said that according to the Bible, the descendants of Abraham would receive land that today would include essentially the entire Middle East, and asked Huckabee if Israel had a right to that land.

Huckabee responded: “It would be fine if they took it all.” Huckabee added, however, that Israel was not looking to expand its territory and has a right to security in the land it legitimately holds.
