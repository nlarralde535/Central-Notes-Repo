## News 9

The House voted against a resolution by Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC) forcing the House Ethics Committee to publicly share all of its investigations into Congressional members accused of sexual harassment or engaging in a sexual relationship with a staffer.

By a vote of 357-65, the House voted to instead refer the issue to the House Ethics Committee itself, essentially killing the effort.

There were 38 Republicans, including Rep. Cory Mills (R-FL) — who faced allegations of sexual harassment from a much younger ex-girlfriend — and 27 Democrats who voted against referring the matter to the House Ethics Committee.
