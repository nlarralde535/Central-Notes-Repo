# CBS 12 WPEC

The Orange County Sheriff's Office announced that for years, Legacy Immigra promised to help undocumented immigrants navigate the complex U.S. immigration system, offering legal assistance in filing claims for asylum and lawful status.

However, authorities said that the agency was anything but legitimate. Instead, it operated as an alleged criminal enterprise that profited by exploiting vulnerable individuals, amassing over $20 million through manipulation, fraud, and extortion.

The investigation, conducted in collaboration with Homeland Security and the Florida Attorney General's Office, identified seven key victims from Florida, South Carolina, Connecticut, and New Jersey, though authorities said they believe many more individuals may have been affected.
