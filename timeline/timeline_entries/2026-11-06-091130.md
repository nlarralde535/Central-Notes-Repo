# KUSI 5 San Diego
San Diego school district leadership and city officials shared on Friday that Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) allegedly took an 8-year-old into custody. 

The San Diego Unified School District (SDUSD), local labor unions and San Diego City Councilmember Sean Elo-Rivera sent a letter to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services and the Office of Refugee Resettlement, alleging ICE of taking an 8-year-old Haitian student to a federal facility earlier this week. 

“Yesterday, a third-grade student from a City Heights school was detained by immigration officials,” Elo-Rivera said in a social media statement on Thursday. “His parents were taken from him last year, and now he’s been taken from his guardian, his school, his friends, and his community.”  

A Department of Homeland Security (DHS) spokesperson said allegations of ICE taking the child into custody to be placed into detention are false.  

“The child was initially illegally brought into the United States with a man who alleged to be his father,” the DHS spokesperson claims. “His father has never been in ICE custody and was previously removed to Mexico by CBP.” 

The spokesperson alleges that the child’s uncle could not prove legal guardianship of the minor and so he was taken into custody, citing Trafficking Victims Protection Reauthorization Act (TVPRA) requirements.  

“Out of an abundance of caution and to ensure the child’s safety, ICE determined it was best to remove the child from the household and place him in the care of the Office of Refugee Resettlement,” the DHS spokesperson said. 

The school district’s letter stated the student was impacted by the Trump administration’s decision to end Temporary Protected Status for Haitian migrants. The decision ended TPS for more than 300,000 Haitians across the U.S.
