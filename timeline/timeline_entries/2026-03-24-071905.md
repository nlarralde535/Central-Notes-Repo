## The Times

The USS Gerald R Ford, the largest warship ever built, has withdrawn from the Middle East to undergo repairs in Greece after the laundry room caught fire, toilets became clogged and reports suggested there were not enough beds for sailors.

The $13 billion aircraft carrier was scrambled to the Middle East when President Trump decided to attack Iran, extending an already lengthy deployment during which it was previously sent to the Caribbean to take part in the overthrowing of Nicolás Maduro, the president of Venezuela.

The 1,106ft long warship has been at sea for nine months and is on course to surpass the record for the longest carrier deployment in American naval history.

Earlier this month, a fire broke out in the ship’s laundry room, resulting in two sailors being treated for lacerations and more than 200 sailors receiving medical aid for smoke inhalation. The fire lasted more than 30 hours and destroyed much of the berthing.
