# MSN
A large group of masked men armed with Confederate flags marched on Capitol Hill on July 4th calling to reclaim the country and getting rid of immigrants.

The shocking images were captured in the nation's capital on America's 250th anniversary.

The group of hundreds of men were marching to the beat of a drum and a clip shared to X showed a streamer with the group describing the march as a “total Aryan victory.”

Reports indicate the men are part of Patriot Front, a white supremacist group that formed in 2017 following the "Unite the Right" rally in Charlottesville, Virginia.

The organization advocates for the creation of a "white ethnostate" and is known for its synchronized flash demonstrations and propaganda campaigns.

# US News & World Report

Hundreds of masked ⁠members ⁠of the white nationalist group Patriot Front ⁠marched through parts of Washington, D.C., on Saturday ahead of Independence Day festivities planned for the evening that have been criticized as divisive. 

The group posted on social media that it had arrived in the capital with about ‌400 members, and Reuters photographers saw hundreds ‌of people dressed in Patriot Front outfits traveling on D.C. Metro trains.

Patriot Front, known for its uniforms, face masks and flash-mob-style demonstrations, formed in 2017 after the deadly "Unite the Right" rally in Charlottesville, Virginia, breaking off from the Vanguard America white supremacist group that was at the center of that protest, according to the Southern Poverty Law Center.

A manifesto on Patriot Front's website says, "Democracy has failed this once great nation," and ⁠a "hard reset" is needed to "return to the traditions and virtues of our forefathers," identifying them as ⁠European settlers.

Patriot Front tries to paint itself as mainstream while touting "white supremacist and anti-immigrant ideological belief systems," said John Cohen, who held various counterterrorism and intelligence roles in the Homeland Security Department during the Obama and Biden administrations.
