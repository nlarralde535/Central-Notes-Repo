# Houston Public Media 
After repeated delays, Houston Mayor John Whitmire's administration is "not taking any action ... at this time" on an application for $150 million from the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ), according to a spokesperson for the mayor's office.

The administration's plan to apply for the DOJ's Model Cities Initiative (MCI) program drew criticism from some members of the public in recent weeks. They were concerned about grant guidelines calling for cities to "ensure that their initiatives align” with a number of priorities, including “immigration law enforcement operations.”

Ten people signed up to speak to the city council about the MCI application during a public comment session on Tuesday evening, including Jennefer Canales-Peláez, senior policy attorney and strategist with the Immigrant Legal Resource Center.

"While I can agree that bringing housing and funding to the city of Houston is important, it should not be done while sacrificing our most vulnerable community members," Canales-Peláez said.

President Donald Trump unveiled the MCI program in June under the banner "Make America Safe Again." He said it was intended to “slash violent crime in major metropolitan areas all across our country.”

The application was slated for consideration by the city council in September, but was delayed twice as city officials sought clarification from the DOJ on the guidelines
