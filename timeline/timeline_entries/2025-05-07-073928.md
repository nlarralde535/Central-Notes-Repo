# KRCR 7 Redding CA
A driver, identified as Jeffrey Bembry, was arrested Tuesday after pointing a rifle at a California Highway Patrol officer during a traffic stop just north of Redding on Interstate 5.

According to the CHP, a car was pulled over on the Mountain Gate off-ramp for speeding when Bembry exited and pointed an AR-style assault rifle at the officer conducting the stop. Officers said Bembry returned to the car and drove off, prompting a pursuit with additional units.

CHP said they pursued Bembry at varying speeds before he exited the freeway at Oasis Road, traveling about two miles west before stopping near Vigil Court.

Bembry was taken into custody without further incident and booked into the Shasta County Jail. No injuries were reported.
