# Israel National News
Israeli Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich said at the Katif Conference on Tuesday that the State of Israel currently has no interest in joining the campaign against Iran.

According to Smotrich, the current situation of a limited confrontation between Iran and the United States is "the most correct and best" situation as far as Israel is concerned.

Smotrich said that Israel's ultimate goal is to "undermine and weaken the regime until iit falls." He added that Israel can not come to terms with a regime that, according to him, aims to destroy Israel and works toward that goal.

He claimed that the best way to bring about the regime's demise is through economic pressure. He said that "we need to crush it economically," and noted that the reinstatement of President Trump's blockade helps with that.

# News 18 India
Israeli Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich has said Israel has “no interest" in joining the renewed US military campaign against Iran, arguing that the current conflict and continued economic pressure on Tehran already serve Israel’s interests.

"Right now, Israel has no interest in joining the conflict. The current situation, where there’s a war between Iran and the US, is actually the best scenario for us," Smotrich said."

His remarks hint that Israel is content with the United States taking the lead in confronting Iran while Israel stays out of direct military action for the time being.

Why Israel may prefer to stay out

Israel’s position indicates that it believes the current US campaign is already weakening Iran without forcing Israel to bear the immediate costs of war.

Strategically, the stance suggests Israel sees an advantage in allowing Washington to maintain military and economic pressure on Tehran while avoiding direct involvement itself.

Joining the conflict could expose Israel to a wider Iranian response, including missile attacks, escalation through Iran-backed proxy groups and increased pressure on energy and shipping routes.

By staying out of the fighting, Israel can conserve military resources, keep its forces on alert and continue preparing contingency plans in case the conflict expands.
