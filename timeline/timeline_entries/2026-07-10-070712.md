# KSAT 12 San Antonio
Two detention officers have been charged with improper sexual activity with an inmate, according to jail records, and the Guadalupe County Sheriff’s Office said a corporal detention officer is also being investigated on related allegations.

The sheriff’s office said it opened the investigation Tuesday after receiving allegations of inappropriate conduct at the Guadalupe County Adult Detention Center.

Andrew Kee and Isaiah Charles were released from jail Saturday, jail records show, and are charged with:
- Violation of civil rights of a person in custody
- Improper sexual activity with a committed person

Kee and Charles were taken into custody by the Texas Rangers, who are leading the investigation.
