# Alarabiya English
The chairman of Yemen’s internationally recognized Presidential Leadership Council, Rashad al-Alimi, said on Monday that “the time has come to end the suffering caused by the terrorist Houthi militia.”

He called on Yemenis from all sections of society to rally behind the state, support the armed forces and security services in their efforts to restore state institutions, and bring an end to the Houthi takeover.

He said the past several years had shown that the tragedy facing Yemenis was not inevitable, but was instead the direct result of the Houthi takeover and the use of civilian suffering to serve the agenda of the Iranian regime and draw Yemen into its regional conflicts.

Al-Alimi renewed his call for residents of provinces under Houthi control to rally behind the state and the republic, describing them as the only guarantee of security, stability and the future all Yemenis deserve.
