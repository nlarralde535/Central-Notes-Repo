# KRIV Fox 26 Houston
A former Webb County sheriff's deputy was sentenced to 20 years in federal prison for sexually assaulting a woman during a traffic stop, federal prosecutors announced.

U.S. District Judge Marina Garcia Marmolejo ordered Ignacio Rodriguez IV, 29, to serve 240 months in federal prison, followed by five years of supervised release. Rodriguez pleaded guilty on April 23 to civil rights violations.

According to the Laredo Morning Times, he joined the agency as a correctional officer in 2021 and was promoted to deputy in November 2023. He resigned from the Webb County Sheriff's Office two days after the incident, reports show.

The investigation was conducted by the FBI and the Laredo Police Department. Rodriguez will remain in custody pending his transfer to a federal prison facility.
