# Clarion Ledger
An arrest was made in connection with the hanging death of 29-year-old Tasia Fortune, Jackson Police Chief RaShall Brackney confirmed Friday, Sept. 11, during a news briefing.

Hinds County Coroner Jeramiah Howard previously told the Clarion Ledger that Fortune was found dead and hanging from a tree in the backyard of a vacant home in the 500 block of Road of Remembrance on Aug. 3 in Jackson.

The Mississippi State Medical Examiner's Office classified Fortune's death as a homicide, Brackney said.

According to Brackney, the Jackson Police Department arrested a man identified as Jarques Ratliff. Brackney said officers arrested Ratliff on Friday, Sept. 11, along Capitol Street in Jackson.

Brackney said Ratliff does have an "association" to Fortune, but the department would not be disclosing the nature of the relationship.

"This is not closure," Brackney said. "This is just one step as we continue to investigate this case, but it is a very important step. As you can tell, the moment that the medical examiner gave us a determination, we were already having the search warrants and the arrest warrants in hand. None of which we could execute until we had a final determination."

Brackney also addressed reports by local media outlets which stated a 65-year-old man, who lived on the same street as the crime scene, was interviewed as a person of interest by investigators.

Local news outlets reported the man admitted he had an argument with Fortune but had no involvement in her death. Police did not charge the man with a crime related to Fortune's death. Police also did not disclose how the man, who was arrested on an unrelated charge of being a felon in possession of a firearm, was connected to Fortune.

"We have never named a person of interest," Brackney said. "That is something that was put out through the media or through the community, but we have never named any person of interest in this case."
