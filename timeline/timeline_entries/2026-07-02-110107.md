# People Magazine
 Former Senate Majority Leader Mitch McConnell, 84, was hospitalized over two weeks ago and his office has offered few details on his condition.

Even after reports surfaced this week that emergency services responded to a call to his Washington, D.C., home for an unconscious person who suffered a possible heart attack on June 14, the Kentucky Republican’s spokespeople declined to address the reports and issued the same sparse statement they did on June 22.

“Senator McConnell is still working closely with staff on Senate business and Kentucky matters as he continues his recovery. However, he will not be voting this week,” spokesperson Stephanie Penn said in the June 22 email.

His office did not respond to a question from PEOPLE about the emergency services dispatch audio that other outlets published on Wednesday, July 1. His staff has also not answered PEOPLE's follow-up questions about why the seven-term senator was hospitalized and whether he is still in the hospital or recovering elsewhere. 

The longtime senator's office confirmed he was hospitalized on June 14 and said in a statement on June 22 that he would not be returning for votes before the Senate left town for a break. McConnell has not voted since June 11, according to Senate records, and the Senate is now on recess until July 13.
