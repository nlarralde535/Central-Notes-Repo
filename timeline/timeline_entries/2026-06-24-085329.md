# Arabian Business
The move was announced by the Oman News Agency, which said the initiative reflects the Sultanate’s responsibility towards the Strait of Hormuz, its strategic importance to the global economy and its commitment to international law and the freedom of navigation.

The temporary corridor will be available to all vessels in accordance with coordinates announced by the IMO and the competent Omani authorities.
