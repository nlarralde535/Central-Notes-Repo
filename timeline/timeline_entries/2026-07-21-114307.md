# ABC 7 New York 
Police say a man armed with a collection of weapons used fireworks to start a fire outside 26 Federal Plaza in Lower Manhattan around 8:30 a.m. Monday.

Andrew Arrabaca doused a stairwell with gasoline, ignited it with a firework and sent flames and smoke up the facade of the building before he fired five to seven pellets from an airsoft gun, the FBI said.

Arrabaca, 43, is a former U.S. Army soldier from 2001-2005, who worked as a mechanic on Patriot missile systems.

"Mr. Arrabaca came armed, with bad intentions," FBI Assistant Director in Charge James Barnacle said.

The Poughkeepsie resident had two axes, a hammer, a machete, and knives in addition to fireworks and gasoline, officials said.

The suspect -- described by the Department of Homeland Security as an "anti-ICE rioter" -- was tackled by a Federal Protective Service police officer, DHS said. He was taken into custody.

"26 Federal Plaza has long been a target of extremist acts of terror. Today, the extremist was brought here," Barnacle said.

Several people suffered minor injuries, including one civilian and two government employees.

"He did say he was targeting the building and did say he was OK with" people getting hurt whether they were federal employees or civilians, Barnacle said.
