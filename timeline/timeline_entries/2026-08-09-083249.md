# Houston Chronicle 
Public records obtained from HISD and compiled by former district parent Tracy Lisewsky show dozens of campuses reached the equivalent of at least half of each school’s listed teaching staff during the 2025-26 school year, adding to concerns about educator turnover three years into the state’s takeover of Texas’ largest school district.

A University of Houston study released in January found teacher retention had already been declining before the state takeover but accelerated afterward. Of HISD’s 11,495 teachers during the 2023-24 school year, 58.6 percent remained at the same campus the following year. Another 12.4 percent moved to schools outside HISD, 7.9 percent left teaching for other public-school jobs and 12 percent left Texas public schools altogether. 

Before the takeover, researchers found HISD retained an average of 74.8 percent of teachers at the same campus each year. 

The study also found surrounding districts and charter networks—including Fort Bend, Katy, Cypress-Fairbanks, Spring Branch and Aldine ISDs—received more former HISD teachers in 2024-25 than during any other year examined going back to 2016-17
