TAG: CBP_ICE

DESCRIPTION 
Department of Homeland Security (DHS) Secretary Kristi Noem has repeatedly claimed that they are arresting the “worst of the worst.” New nonpublic data from Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) leaked to the Cato Institute reveal a different story.

Of people booked into ICE custody this fiscal year (since October 1, 2025):
-  Nearly three in four (73 percent) had no criminal conviction.
- Nearly half had no criminal conviction nor even any pending criminal charges.
