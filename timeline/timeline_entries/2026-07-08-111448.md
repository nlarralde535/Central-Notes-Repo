# Houston Chronicle 
A handful of armed U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents surrounded a house in Spring on Wednesday morning after a man barricaded himself in a home on Saint Winfred Drive.

Around 8:06 a.m., the Harris County Sheriff's Office received a call from a man who identified himself as an ICE agent and said a suspect with weapon charges had fled and crashed into a home, according to the law enforcement agency. The single-family home is located in The Chancel subdivision off TC Jester near Spring Cypress.

Officials at the scene said a man had barricaded himself in the house after a chase. At around 10:50 a.m., a man was seen at the door of the home talking to agents. A man came out of the house and was detained and taken away around 11:15 a.m. A person inside the house declined to comment. 

On Wednesday afternoon, a federal felony complaint identified the man as Jorge Erick Lopez De La Pena. According a federal felony complaint, De La Pena is a Mexican national who was accused of re-entering the United States without permission after being previously deported. De La Pena had no listed defense attorney as of Wednesday afternoon.
