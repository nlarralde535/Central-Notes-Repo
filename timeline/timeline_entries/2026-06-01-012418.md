# Spectrum News
A Shelby officer was charged with assault Monday after being fired over the weekend following a violent arrest that the city’s police chief called “disturbing and inappropriate.”

Footage captured by a door camera Friday and obtained by CNN appears to show the officer punching a woman during a tussle on the ground before another officer intervenes and helps to get handcuffs on her. She says repeatedly in the video that she does not have a warrant.  

“As seen in the video currently circulating on social media, the actions taken by one officer during that encounter are disturbing and inappropriate,” Police Chief Brad Fraser said Friday in a statement. “A second officer can be seen intervening to stop those actions.”

 Officer Karson Hyder was charged with assault inflicting serious injury, the North Carolina State Bureua of Investigation said Monday. Hyder turned himself in to the Cleveland County Detention Center and was released on $10,000 secured bond.

The SBI is looking into an allegation of excessive use of force at the request of the police chief and the Cleveland County district attorney.

Fraser said Hyder, who had been placed on administrative suspension Friday, was fired Saturday morning after a department review.
