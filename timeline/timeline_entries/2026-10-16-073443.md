# Police 1 
A Savannah Police officer was fired for sharing her ALPR system credentials with her ICE officer husband, WJCL reported.

In department files obtained by WJCL, the Savannah PD reported that it fired the officer after concluding that she gave an unauthorized person access to a sensitive police system, violated policy and compromised its use-tracking safeguards. 

The officer’s husband did not have a Flock account through the Department of Homeland Security. However, he was able to conduct nearly 1,000 searches across six states related to ICE investigations between January and July 2026, according to the audit. The ICE officer estimated he used the account for about four months during that time frame.

The ICE officer denied using the system for any personal reason and stated that every search was for a person with an active arrest warrant, WJCL reported. The investigation began after an internal audit flagged the Savannah officer’s Flock use as unusually high. 

The investigation did not find that the Savannah officer’s misconduct violated Georgia state law regarding ALPRs. The disciplinary records obtained by WJCL show that the officer appealed the termination decision, saying she was trying to assist another law enforcement agency. The police chief’s office agreed with the recommendation for her termination on Aug. 13.
