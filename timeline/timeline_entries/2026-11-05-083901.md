# KSBS 7 Denver

A Lakewood police agent was killed and three other agents and a bystander were injured in a shooting on an RTD bus Thursday evening.

According to Lakewood police, a suspect from an earlier hit-and-run near Kipling and West 13th Avenue was spotted boarding the bus near the area of West Colfax Ave. and Wadsworth Blvd. around 6:30 p.m.

Agents came into contact with the suspect on the bus, and he opened fire on them. The agents fired back killing the suspect, officials said.

According to the department, police agent Nicholas Graham, 33, died from his injuries; three other agents were injured in the shooting as well, described as serious to critical injuries. One civilian was also injured in the incident. The extent of their injuries is not known.

Multiple informed sources speaking to Denver7 Investigates identified the alleged shooter as Gabriel Steven Garcia, 34.
