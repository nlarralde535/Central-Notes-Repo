# Palestine Chronicle
Key Developments

    The IRGC said it launched missile and drone strikes against the US Fifth Fleet headquarters and other American military positions.
    Tehran said the operation was a response to US attacks on an Iranian oil tanker and a communications tower on Qeshm Island.
    Explosions were reported near Qeshm Island, while military activity and air defense operations were reported in Kuwait and Bahrain.
