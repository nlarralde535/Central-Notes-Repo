# CBS Austin
Bastrop police have identified a suspect accused of opening fire in a Home Depot parking lot Wednesday afternoon, an incident that prompted a large law enforcement response but left no reported injuries.

Police Chief said preliminary information indicates the suspect, identified as 25-year-old Kobe Lee Mosser, drove into the parking lot and began firing an AR-15-style rifle "indiscriminately." At one point, Mosser exited his vehicle and continued shooting, officials said.

# Fox 7 Austin
Court records show Mosser has a prior criminal history, including a 2023 family violence charge in Bastrop County and a DWI in 2022.

Records also show previous arrests in Montgomery County for DWI in 2017 and resisting arrest in 2020, both cases resulting in guilty pleas.
