# Houston Chronicle
U.S. Secretary of Energy Chris Wright issued an emergency order effective through Sunday after the Southwest Power Pool Inc. (SPP) requested federal authority to help relieve grid stress caused by extreme heat and other factors. The order covers 17 states, including Texas.

"The order directs the Southwest Power Pool...to dispatch specified generation units and to order their operation as needed to maintain reliability," according to a news release from the U.S. Department of Energy. "The order also authorizes SPP to direct backup generation resources to operate as a last resort before declaring an Energy Emergency Alert (EEA) 3 or during an EEA 3."

Parts of the state served by SPP could temporarily tap backup generators and additional power plants as the federal government tries to reduce the risk of heat-related power outages. The order also specifically authorizes SPP to require backup generation resources at data centers, including hyperscale facilities, and other large industrial and commercial sites.

"SPP expects employment of these backup generation resources will reduce stress on the grid and permit orderly, safe, and secure operations during the hot weather conditions and other causes," the order reads.
