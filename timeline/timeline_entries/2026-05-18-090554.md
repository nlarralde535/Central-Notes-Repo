# The Hill
The U.S. has again rejected an Iranian proposal to end the conflict between the two countries, according to a new report. 

Axios reported Monday that Tehran offered a revised peace proposal that the White House shot down, per a top American official and another source close to the matter.

Reuters also reported Monday that the Iranians had transmitted a fresh peace proposal to Washington, featuring terms seemingly close to those that the Americans have rejected in the past.

Reuters also reported that Pakistan sent the Iranian proposal to the U.S., per a Pakistani source.

On Sunday, President Trump advised Iran that the “clock is ticking” as peace talks between Washington and Tehran seemed to be at a standstill.

“For Iran, the Clock is Ticking, and they better get moving, FAST, or there won’t be anything left of them. TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE,” Trump said on Truth Social.

Trump last week declined an Iranian response to a White House peace proposal, referring to it as “totally unacceptable” after the Iranians requested separating nuclear talks from peace discussions
