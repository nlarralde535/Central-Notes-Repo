# ABC News
A top general at the Pentagon was suddenly removed from his senior post earlier this week for allegedly leaking classified information, according to a senior Pentagon official.

Lt. Gen. Brett Sylvia served in the Joint Staff as the Director for Strategy, Plans & Policy for 11 months, an important office that handles long-term strategic planning and that is known in military jargon as the J-5.

Sylvia was removed from the position for a lack of confidence, a source told ABC News. However, new information in a statement from a senior Pentagon to ABC News said Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth fired Sylvia because an inquiry determined that he had allegedly been leaking classified information to the press about the war with Iran, the war in Ukraine and other matters.

The official said Hegseth fired Sylvia for “severe misconduct,” that he took “immediate action,” and that an ongoing investigation has been referred to the Justice Department.

Sean Parnell, the Pentagon's top spokesman, confirmed in a social media post on Thursday that "Following an extensive Air Force OSI investigation, the Department took necessary personnel action to protect sensitive information" and that the matter has been referred to the Justice Department for "potential further legal action." Parnell never identifies Sylvia as the subject of that investigation. 

In an earlier statement, a spokesperson for the Joint Staff expressed their "deepest gratitude" for Sylvia's "distinguished" service and said they "wish him and his family the best," but later issued a new statement.

"We are withdrawing our earlier statement concerning this personnel matter," the new statement said. "Upon further review, we determined that the statement did not appropriately reflect the full circumstances. Out of respect for the servicemember and the ongoing personnel process, we will not comment further."

The J-5 is recognized as one of the Joint Staff's most important directorates in the Joint Staff along with the directorate for Operations, the J-3, that is responsible for the planning and execution of military operations.

# Military.com
On Oct. 29, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth fired former Army Lt. Gen. Brett Sylvia and accused him of leaking classified information about the Iran War and Russia-Ukraine war to the media.

Now, according to three anonymous sources speaking to Axios on Tuesday, the Pentagon is investigating Sylvia further and has linked his leaks to the Wall Street Journal and New York Times.

The sources that spoke to Axios claimed that Sylvia shared information with Michael Gordon of the Wall Street Journal, and Greg Jaffe of the New York Times in the fall of 2025. Sylvia allegedly discussed information about the two conflicts, as well as Hegseth's firing of military leaders.

A legal analysis of Sylvia's conduct also alleged that the former general discussed "munitions stockpiles and high-level inter-agency discussions with Iran," one source told Axios.

The sources shared an internal Pentagon report which showed Sylvia failed four polygraphs and admitted to leaking to the media in two video interviews.

"Major General Sylvia relied on notes he took while reviewing a classified secret report to orally brief Gordon on assistance to Ukraine," the Pentagon report said, adding that the authorities did not give Sylvia clearance to discuss this information.

When asked about Sylvia’s firing on a tarmac last week, President Trump misidentified Sylvia as a woman. “She’s going to have big problems, long-term problems,” he said.
