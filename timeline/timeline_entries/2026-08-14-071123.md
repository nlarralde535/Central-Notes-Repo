# The Detroit News
Self-described anti-Islamic activist Jake Lang said he expects trouble when he and his supporters come to Dearborn Tuesday for a planned rally and pig roast — a dig at Muslims, whose religion eschews eating pork.

Dearborn police and city officials said they hope Lang is instead greeted with silence. For security reasons, city officials have moved the City Council meeting from City Hall to the Henry Ford Centennial Library, away from the planned rally site about a half-mile west of the library.

The scheduled visit is part of an ongoing anti-Muslim campaign by Lang, who has had previous run-ins in Dearborn, including an incident last year in which competing groups of protesters clashed. Following the fracas, Lang said he'd filed a federal lawsuit against the city, mayor and police chief, claiming they'd failed to protect him.

Multiple Dearborn stakeholders are pleading with people to refrain from engaging with the self-professed anti-Muslim activist when he makes his scheduled visit Tuesday.
