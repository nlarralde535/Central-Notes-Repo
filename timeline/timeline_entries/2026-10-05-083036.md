# WTVJ Miami
A man who was wearing U.S. Customs and Border Enforcement attire at Miami-Dade College while voting for Brazil's general election was underway was arrested on Sunday, deputies said.

Danilo Silva, 46, was charged with voter intimidation, conspiracy to commit bribery and unlawful use of a police badge, records showed.

According to the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office, detectives, alongside Homeland Security, were at the polling site when several people saw Silva, who was walking around aggressively while wearing a blue ICE hat, sunglasses, a mask covering his face, a blue ICE raid jacket and boots.

An arrest report said Silva was walking from one voting location to another and was engaging multiple people. The people who were gathered were in fear because Silva was portraying himself as ICE and was interfering with their election.

Silva was then approached by two men, with one holding a camera, and they confronted him about what he was wearing and that he was scaring voters, the report said. Silva then responded aggressively, and detectives intervened.

Silva, the report said, then told detectives that the ICE attire was a costume that he bought on Amazon and that he wasn't breaking the law by wearing it and being at the voting location.

He told detectives that he was there to vote, and when he finished, he switched his Brazilian jersey for the ICE attire, and wanted to see people's reaction, the report said.
