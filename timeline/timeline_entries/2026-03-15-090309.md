Senator Lindsey Graham says we are engaged in a religious war against radical Islam

++DESCRIPTION++
At the end of the day, Lindsey Graham got his war. The scariest aspect of this tale is not that he lobbied on matters of war and peace (something senators often do) but that his lobbying worked, and that his understanding of the Middle East bears almost no connection to reality.

What comes after, Graham insists, is up to the Iranian people. What that means exactly, no one in Washington knows, not even the senator.
