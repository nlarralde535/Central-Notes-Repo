# WNBC 4 New York
On Thursday, Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents shot and wounded an undocumented immigrant with a criminal past as, they say, he attempted to flee the scene with a 5-year-old child in the back seat of his car, officials said.

The shooting occurred during an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) operation on Marble Hill Avenue around 4 p.m., the sources said.

One source indicated that the person shot, identified by law enforcement sources as Oscar Belgal, was apparently inside a vehicle when at least one agent opened fire. Belgal is expected to survive.

DHS said as ICE-ERO officers tried to box in his car and make the arrest, Belgal “rammed” the ICE vehicles.

But NYC Police Commissioner Jessica Tisch said Belgal was pulling into a parking space when a federal officer in plainclothes approached with a gun drawn, followed by another federal officer who also wasn't in uniform. Belgal started to pull the car forward, and then at least one of the officers fired seven rounds, hitting the man below his left ear, Tisch said at a news conference near the shooting site late Thursday.

The 5-year-old child was not hurt and is in relatives' custody, one official said.

Department of Homeland Security officials allege Belgal is connected to the Trinitarios street gang. They said he has numerous past arrests for robbery, drugs, and other crimes
