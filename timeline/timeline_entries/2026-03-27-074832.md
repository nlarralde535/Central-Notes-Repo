++TITLE++
Colorado sheriff resigns after 5 members of office indicted by DA's office

++DESCRIPTION++
Five members of a southern Colorado sheriff's office, including the now-former sheriff and undersheriff, have been indicted by a grand jury following two separate incidents, one involving the treatment of human remains and one involving use of force. 

Former Costilla County Sheriff Danny Sanchez and former Deputy Keith Schultz were each charged with four counts of official misconduct and one count of abuse of a corpse due to their handling of unidentified human remains found on a rural property in 2024.
