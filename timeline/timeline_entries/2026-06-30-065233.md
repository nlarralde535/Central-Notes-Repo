# New Media Detroit
Investigators identified the man as Jerard R. Jackson, who was last seen at approximately 10:00 a.m. EDT on Monday after leaving the Electric Forest Festival in Rothbury.

According to Michigan State Police, troopers initially responded to Jackson’s campsite earlier that morning after he reported an alleged criminal incident involving a friend. Troopers interviewed everyone involved and conducted an investigation at the campsite. Police said the investigation found no evidence that a criminal incident had occurred at the festival.

After speaking with troopers, Jackson left the festival grounds alone. Authorities said he left behind his cellular phone and other personal belongings.

As the investigation continued, the rental vehicle Jackson had been driving was located in the Rothbury area. The vehicle was found unoccupied, with Jackson’s wallet and keys still inside.

Michigan State Police said Jackson was later located deceased in a wooded area near where the rental vehicle had been found.

Based on the investigation, authorities said it appears Jackson died by suicide. Police said there is no indication of foul play. The investigation remains ongoing pending the final report from the medical examiner.
