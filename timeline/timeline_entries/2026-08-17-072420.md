# WDHN Webb Alabama
Former Penn State University students and fraternity members were arrested as part of “Operation Drugs Unlimited,” Pennsylvania Attorney General Dave Sunday announced.

A Pennsylvania Grand Jury indicted 14 people, 13 of whom were PSU students during the 2023/2024 school year when cocaine was allegedly being trafficked from New York and Philadelphia into State College, PA.

While the trafficking took place in 2023 and 2024, the arrests came recently after a thorough investigation. Sunday explained that investigators wanted to arrest everyone connected with the trafficking organization at once, which took time.

“This was a coordinated and highly-profitable drug-trafficking crew revolving around two senior members of the fraternities, other fraternity brothers, and pledges,” Attorney General Sunday said. “In fact, according to the evidence uncovered, cutting and packaging cocaine was, for some pledges, an indoctrination into the fraternities.”
