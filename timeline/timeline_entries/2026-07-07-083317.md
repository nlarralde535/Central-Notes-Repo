# KHOU
Federal officials said a man was shot and killed after trying to run over an ICE agent near the intersection of Canal and Wayside on Tuesday morning.

A man was shot and killed Tuesday morning by federal agents in Houston's East End, according to United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement officials. The victim's family is now disputing ICE's account of what happened.

ICE officials said the incident unfolded just before 7 a.m. near the intersection of Canal Street and Wayside Drive.

An ICE spokesperson said officers were trying to pull over a vehicle as part of a "targeted enforcement operation." They said the driver of the vehicle they tried to pull over, Lorenzo Salgado Araujo, tried to get away. ICE officials said Araujo is here illegally from Mexico.



Investigators said Araujo rammed into an ICE vehicle and refused to follow orders. They also said he tried to "weaponize" his vehicle and run over an officer.

Federal officials said the officer opened fire in self-defense during the incident, and Araujo was struck. He was taken to an area hospital, where he was later pronounced dead.

Surveillance video obtained by KHOU 11 from a nearby business shows a white van traveling on Wayside before turning left onto Canal Street. Two dark SUVs appear behind it, one passing the van and one staying behind it. The van then makes a U-turn and pulls over alongside a business, where one of the SUVs stops next to it.

Houston Mayor John Whitmire declined to comment on the incident.
