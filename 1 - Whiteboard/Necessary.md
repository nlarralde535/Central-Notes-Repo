# The Script
1. Establish that you want to exchange get-to-know-you questions in order to save time
2. Establish where she would prefer to communicate: in the app, on the phone, in-person, some other alternative 
3. State a bit **About Me** and **What I'm Looking For** 
4. Start asking questions...
    -  how do you show interest, or how do you demonstrate that you care about someone?
    - what are you looking for?
    - What is your philosophy of/attitude towards relationships? 
    - What are some of your most important boundaries?
    - what are some of the red flags you look for?
    - What are some things that you love about yourself? 
    - What are some things that you don't like about yourself or are trying to improve? 
    - why did you match with me?
    - What are you working on these days?
    - What is occupying your attention these days?
    - Do you enjoy being active/physical activities? (will you play with me?)
    - What does demonstrating interest, or being intentional look like to you?
    - (IG) If you were in a room full of everyone you've ever met, who would you look for first?
    - (IG) Given a box of everything you ever lost, what's the 1st thing you'd look for?
    - (IG) Suppose I were to do/say something you didn't like, how would you handle that?
