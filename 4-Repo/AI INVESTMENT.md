# Description 
Are we to believe that the capital flowing into AI today is the best use thereof, and of the resources that those capital flows imply? There seems to be alot of "trust me bro..." baked into such arguments. Why should we trust proponents of capitalism when they say "just trust us, it will all work out" when they extend no such trust to anyone else? Compare the AI build out to the "dark fiber" of the Dot Com bubble, and to the market crash of 1873 caused by the rush to build railroads.
