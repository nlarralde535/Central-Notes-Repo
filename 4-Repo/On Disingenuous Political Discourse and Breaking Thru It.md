# Thesis:
The first half of the 2nd Trump administration is characterized by small-minded, ignorant, racist policies that months later are getting overturned (partially or in totality) by the courts. That is, lives are upturned and destroyed for months while lawyers for affected parties being lawsuits and administration lawyers plead their case before judges. 

It is only in these courtrooms, when standing before a judge, that representatives of the administration are forced to say what they really mean, and to confront the brutal reality of they policies they mean to implement.

The Legislative and Executive branches appear to be fully empowered to ignore reality or the simple meaning of words, and to all but openly lie about what they are doing and intend to do. And we as citizens are only able to watch from afar and hope that the Judicial branch has the mettle to hold the other branches to account.

Meanwhile, at the level of the individual citizen, I have experienced first-hand assurances from people in defence of the administration claiming that Trump and his people do not really mean the horrible things they say, that they are just "trolling".

Even if this were true, which it is not, the seriousness of political decision-making demands seriousness on the part of elected officials. And I believe this demand extends to the voting public as well. The voting public has a responsibility to understand all of the issue at play, and to have defensible arguments therefore. And this is merely a prerequisite for subsequent action. Action in the form of the citizenry engaging in political arguments amongst each other. 

When it comes to such political Discourse, for my part, all benefit of the doubt is exhausted. People are to be taken at their word and treated accordingly. 

To be clean, freedom of speech is to remain protected. But I will not consider "speech" to be voicing half-baked political opinions that call for direct, unnecessary, capricious harm towards others. I see no need to "wait and see" the real-world impact of such poorly-articulated and ultimately indefensible positions. Take people at their word, and treat them accordingly. Treat them to the violence they intend to do to others. This will force people to be clear in their political speech and clear in their political intentions. 

The voting public is perfectly capable of policing itself.
