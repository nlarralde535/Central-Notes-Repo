## [Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation electrician laws and rules](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/electricians/laws-rules.htm)

Texas Electrical Safety & Licensing Act 2003
- Sec. 1305.152. License Required
- Sec 1305.155. Journeyman Electrician
    - must have 8000 hours work experience 
    - must pass licensing exam
- Sec. 1305.161. Apprentice
    - must be 18 years of age

Certificates: 
- A certificate is a formal award granted by an institution of higher education (IHE) certifying the satisfactory completion of a higher education program
- Level I certificate: completing a program of 15-42 semester credit hours
- Level II certificate: completing a program of 30-51 semester credit hours
