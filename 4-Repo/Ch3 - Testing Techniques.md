# Lesson 48: Testing combines techniques that focus on testers (people), coverage, potential problems, activities, and evaluation 
Five-fold Testing System: any testing that you do can be described in terms of 5 dimensions
- TESTERS- who does the testing?
- COVERAGE - what gets tested?
- PROBLEMS - why is it being tested? What risks are being tested for?
- ACTIVITIES - how will testing be performed?
- EVALUATION - determine when a test passes or fails
All testing involves all 5 dimensions. A given testing technique focuses your attention on one or a few dimensions while leaving the others open to your judgement.

Individual testing techniques don't necessarily fit in only one dimension. Not should they; all testing involves all 5 dimensions. ... By keeping all 5 dimensions in mind as you test you might make better choices of combinations.
