When all tests are green and you're faced with the next piece of functionality to implement, here's how TDD says to proceed:
 - WRONG: "How do I implement X?"
 - RIGHT: "How should I test for X?"

- Your imagination for a given implementation is limited by your imagination for a corresponding test.
