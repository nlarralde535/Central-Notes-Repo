# DESCRIPTION 
This is for the purpose of historical context regarding the Constitutional Convention, as well as our effort to recall a quote/statement by George Washington related to "patriotism" not being sufficient to maintain enlistment numbers in the Continental Army.

# NOTES

## Disk 3
19:00
"We have consulted our wishes rather than our reason in the indulgence of an idea of accommodation" - Nathaniel Green in response to King George III's speech to parliament declaring his intention to crush the American colonial rebellion by force, which was delivered in October of 1775, and the first copies of which arrived in the colonies in January of 1776.

8:50
Regarding the state of the army in the fall/winter of 1775:
"But re-enlistments were alarmingly few. Of...roughly 10000 men, fewer than 1000 had agreed to stay." - McCullough 
"Some stimulus besides love of country must be found to make men want to serve" - George Washington
