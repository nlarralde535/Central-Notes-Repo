# Terms
- strategic interdependence
    - "what I do depends on what you do, or what I `think` you're gonna do
- strictly dominated strategy 
- mutual vs. individual interests
- Nash equilibrium
    - "revealing your opponents strategy does not lead you to want to change your own strategy"
- payoff matrix
- best responses
    - more or less the same thing as a Nash Equilibrium
