Show your work. The point of asking these questions is to demonstrate that the respondent probably cannot substantiate to a reasonable degree their answers. 

1. Who won the 2020 US presidential election?
2. What brought an end to the Vietnam War?
3. What brought an end to the Pacific war in WWII?
4. Describe the hand gesture Elon Musk made on January 20, 2025 while speaking at a rally after Trump's second inauguration 
5. What caused the 1965 Watts Riots?
6. What caused the 1992 LA Riots?
