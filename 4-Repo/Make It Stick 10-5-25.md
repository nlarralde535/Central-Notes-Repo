***Generation*** is the cognitive activity of trying to solve a problem, or answer a question, rather than being presented with the solution, or the information. ... When you're asked to supply an answer or a solution to something that's new to you, the power of generation to aid in learning is more evident. ... Unsuccessful attempts to solve a problem encourage deep processing of the answer when it is later supplied"

***Reflection*** is the cognitive activity of taking time to review what has been learned from a given experience and asking yourself questions about it. Reflection can involve other cognitive activities liked to improved learning benefits: retrieval, elaboration, generation. 

Page 90
Failure is a part of learning, not apart from learning. Providing corrective feedback is key for the learner who is doing the difficult work of trying to foster deep learning (spacing, interleaving, varying). Reflecting on, elaborating, and generating thoughts about a subject one is learning, without getting confirmation that one's reflections are either accurate or inaccurate, is useless.
