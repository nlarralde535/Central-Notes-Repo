# Thesis:
The Milei Administration's policies have reduced inflation dramatically in the short term, but Milei is facing the real possibility of electoral failure during national elections in October 2025. This failure is due to the pain caused to individual Argentine citizens by Milei's fiscal policy cuts to pensions and other entitlements. 

Even if reducing government spending is considered a good thing, convincing voters to allow their own benefits to be cut is difficult. 

The US has extended Argentina a $20B swap line to support the Argentine peso, and reduce the negative impact of Milei's policies. 

Republicans need Milei to succeed in Argentina so they can promote their own spending cut policies at home.
