Page 178
IQ/intelligence for each individual has some fixed ceiling, same as with any other biological characteristic (height, athletic ability, etc), but there exist cognitive ***multipliers*** that can be used to "amp up" and get the most out of the intelligence one already possesses: 
 - growth mindset
 - practicing like an expert (deliberate practice)
 - employing memory cues/mnemonic devices
