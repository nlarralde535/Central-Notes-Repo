Page 82
"Effortful recall of learning...requires that you reload or reconstruct components of the skill or material anew from long-term memory rather than mindlessly repeating them from short-term memory. ... The more effort that is required to recall a memory or execute a skill, provided that effort succeeds, the more that effort will benefit learning. ... Massed practice gives us the warm sensation of mastery because we're looping information through short-term memory"

"Conceptual learning" relates to learning that bolsters one's ability to identify both similarities and differences between given examples. "It is thought that this heightened sensitivity to similarities and differences during interleaved practice leads us to encoding of more complex and nuanced representations of the study material."
