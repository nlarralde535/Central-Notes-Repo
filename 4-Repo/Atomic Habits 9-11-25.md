***Pointing-and-calling*** safety system used by Japanese public railway workers is used to make workers more conscious of the details that make up their jobs in order to more easily identify when something is not right and thus prevent accidents. 
"... it raises the level of awareness form a non conscious habit to a more conscious level"

Page 70
***Implementation Intentions*** are explicit plans one makes before engaging in an act/behavior, which detail WHEN and WHERE the act/behavior will be done. 
"Implementation intentions leverage the cues of time and location, the two most common cues that trigger habits"
We did this ^ by linking brushing our teeth with leaving the house. 

Page 73
"No behavior happens in isolation. Each action becomes a cue that triggers the next behavior"

Page 74
***Habit Stacking*** is a special firm of an Implementation Intentions where you attach a desired new habit to an exiting habit, instead of simply to some predetermined time and location. 
"After \[CURRENT HABIT\] I will do \[NEW HABIT\]"

Page 78
"Habit Stacking works best when the cue is highly specific and immediately actionable. ... Habits like 'read more' and 'eat better' are worthy causes, but these goals do not provide sufficiently specific instructions on how and when to act."
