**I believe** that most Americans hold political beliefs that are logically inconsistent, or beliefs that they have spent little to no time closely examining. 

I believe that these same Americans THINK that they understand their political beliefs thoroughly, and thoroughly understand why they hold them.

I believe that since the US is a democratic republic, and a representative democracy, this means that the policies and actions of the US Federal government are a direct representation of the will and wishes of (at minimum) the voting public. 

What this ^ means is that the richest and most powerful nation on earth is being steered by captains who THINK they understand navigation, but in actual practical terms they do not, because they don't really have to.

I believe there exists a class of US citizens who have made it their job to assume the responsibility of making and carrying-out these navigation decisions. 

I believe the true captains (the voting public) have ceded far too much decision-making responsibility to this ^ class of citizens. 

I believe I do not fully understand how/why this ^ ceding has happened.

I believe the average American has a responsibility to consider their political beliefs very carefully, due to the outsized impact their government has on world events. 

I believe Americans should be made to feel the real-world consequences of their political beliefs, and of their abdication of the responsibility of participating meaningfully in their system of government. 

I believe the average American should pay far more attention to the policies and actions of their government both at home and abroad. 

I believe I do not have all of the answers to the question "how should a society govern itself". 

I believe I have a good set of tools for assessing/analyzing suggested answers to the question "how should a society govern itself". 

---

I want input from my fellow Americans in regards to answering the question "how should a society govern itself?", since I can't answer that question all by myself .

I want to challenge the confidence with which the average American holds their political beliefs. 

I want to make the average American aware of the inconsistencies in many of the political beliefs they hold. 

I want to highlight to the average American the lack of scrutiny applied to many of these ^ beliefs by the average American.

I want to make the average American aware of the consequences suffered by people they will never meet, due to the policies and actions of the US government. 

I want to hold the average American responsible for the policies and actions of their government. 

I want to physically punish those Americans who would resolutely cling to their ignorance once the consequences of their ignorance, and the consequences of their incoherent political beliefs have been made clear to them.

I want to physically punish the average American who proudly holds selfish, self-centered political beliefs.
