There are a handful of pushup variations that we do: 
 - Traditional pushups
 - Archer Pushups 
 - 1-arm pushups
 - Intrinsic Strength pushups 

## Sets & Reps:
Traditional pushups
 - n sets of 12 @ 1 minute intervals
Archer Pushups 
 - n sets of 3, alternating sides
1-arm pushups
 - n sets of 5R -> 5L
Intrinsic Strength pushups
 - n sets of 5R -> 5L
