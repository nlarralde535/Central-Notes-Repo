***Temptation Bundling*** is when you "bundle" and activity that you want/are inclined to do with another activity that you should/have to do. 
"Temptation Bundling is a way of applying a psychology theory called Premack's Principle, which states that 'more probably behaviors will reinforce less probable behaviors'.
