## Steps
 - jack up the front end so wheels are free
 - fill res to max line KEEP RES FULL
 - connect vacuum pump adapter to res
 - elevate the fluid/vapor separator above the res
 - pull 20 inHg of vacuum - notice air bubbles coming thru the tube
 - start the engine
 - turn the steering wheel lock to lock 10 limes to get more air out CHECK THE RES FLUID LEVEL EVERY FEW TURNS - notice the pump get quieter and quieter as you do this
 - turn off car
 - check fluid level in res again
 - Repeat
