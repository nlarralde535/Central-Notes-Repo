# Compulsory Voting
I believe that compulsory voting would greatly reduce the rates of malignant incumbency we see amongst elected officials today. If every elected official had to convince a full majority/plurality of their entire electorate every single election, and could not count on low turnout to see them thru to victory, that would 1. make elections far more competitive, and 2. make the corps of elected officials more closely represent the voting public. 

# Compulsory Public Service
I believe that citizens should engage in public service of some kind as a matter of course. That is, service rendered towards one's local community. Something like 1-2 years after highschool, serving either with the local police, or withing the county court system, or the corrections system, or public utility departments.
